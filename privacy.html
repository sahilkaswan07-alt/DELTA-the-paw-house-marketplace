4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and display your listings
  • To operate search, filters, saved listings, and the verification-badge system
  • To enable Buyers and Sellers to contact each other about a listing
  • To respond to support requests and grievances
  • To detect and prevent fraud, fake listings, and misuse of the Platform
  • To send service-related notifications (e.g., verification status updates)
  • To improve the Platform's design, performance, and features
  • To comply with applicable law or respond to lawful requests from authorities

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Legal Basis / Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (given when you create an account, submit a listing, or otherwise use the Platform), to perform our role as a marketplace connecting you with other Users, and to comply with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us, subject to Section 9 (Data Retention).

6. How We Share Information

  • With other Users — your listing content and any contact details you choose to include are visible to Buyers/Sellers so a transaction can be arranged directly between you.
  • With service providers — hosting, database, authentication, image storage, and similar technical providers who process data on our behalf, strictly to operate the Platform (see Section 7).
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Delta, our Users, or the public (for example, in response to a report of a fake listing, fraud, or illegal wildlife trade).
  • Business transfers — if Delta is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Third-Party Services We Use

The Platform relies on third-party infrastructure providers to operate, which may process data on our behalf, including:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database (Firestore), and hosting
  • Cloudinary — storage and delivery of listing photos

These providers process data under their own security and privacy commitments as our data processors. We may update this list as our technical stack changes; material changes will be reflected in this Policy.

8. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies or similar local-storage technologies to keep you signed in, remember filters/preferences, and understand basic usage patterns. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some Platform features (such as staying logged in).

9. Data Retention

We retain account and listing information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to resolve disputes, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 11); some information may be retained where required by law or legitimate business need (e.g., records of a reported fraudulent listing).

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards (such as authentication controls and access restrictions) to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

11. Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data

  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 16. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required by applicable law.
